# gmgn radio — Visual Concept v2

## Direction

Reduce the visual effects from the first concept and make the DJ presence feel
like a quiet, native macOS ambient utility. The desktop should remain usable,
while the breathing sphere communicates state through restrained motion.

## Generation prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit.

Edit the existing gmgn radio desktop product concept board while preserving its
overall 16:9 composition, macOS desktop context, small "gmgn radio" identity,
and the five-state strip labeled IDLE, LISTENING, SPEAKING, MUSIC-REACTIVE,
IMMERSIVE.

Make the visual effects dramatically more restrained and product-like:

- Reduce bloom, nebula, particles, cosmic smoke, sparks, and luminous energy by
  roughly 80%.
- Replace the large glossy magic orb with a smaller, calm semi-matte translucent
  breathing sphere. No bright core, no floor reflection, no explosive aura.
- IDLE: nearly still, one faint soft halo.
- LISTENING: two subtle inward rings, restrained cyan edge.
- SPEAKING: small voice-like ripples near the sphere surface.
- MUSIC-REACTIVE: delicate edge deformation and slow color breathing only, no
  particle burst.
- IMMERSIVE: a low-contrast full-screen shader field made of sparse flowing
  bands and blurred spectral membranes, not a galaxy plume.
- Use deep neutral charcoal, desaturated indigo, faint cyan and violet, with one
  tiny warm accent.
- Preserve generous negative space so the desktop remains usable. The product
  should feel like a quiet native macOS ambient utility, refined and editorial.

Avoid ChatGPT or OpenAI logos, galaxy imagery, nebula explosions, magic spells,
sparks, lens flares, glossy chrome, oversized bloom, dramatic ground
reflections, equalizer bars, excessive UI cards, and illegible labels.
